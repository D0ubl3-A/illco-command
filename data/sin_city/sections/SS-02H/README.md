# SS-02H — Maryland Parkway / Rochelle Avenue corridor

This cycle releases only source-confirmed section identity, building identities, parking relationships, semantic edges, and partial architecture facts for Maryland Administrative Buildings 1 and 2.

## Released

- South Maryland Parkway / East Rochelle Avenue corridor identity
- Maryland Administrative Building 1 identity, address, one-floor record, 9,879 gross square feet, and documented use
- Maryland Administrative Building 2 identity, address, one-floor record, 37,755 gross square feet, and documented use
- Ten explicit semantic relationships with resolvable source references
- MAB1 and MAB2 parking-context relationships

## Blocked

No section polygon, coordinates, APNs, parcel boundaries, rights-of-way, road geometry, terrain samples, building footprints, roof/facade geometry, materials, sprites, models, or aligned placement are released. Street addresses are identity evidence only and are not treated as surveyed model origins.

## Sources

Primary identity and architecture evidence is from official University of Nevada, Las Vegas campus records. Clark County GIS and USGS 3DEP are registered as required future geometry and terrain sources.
