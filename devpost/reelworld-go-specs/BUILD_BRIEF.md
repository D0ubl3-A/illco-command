## Concept
A short, spatial fishing mini-game for Snap SPECS. A virtual fishing spot appears anchored in the user's surroundings. The player casts, waits for a bite, sets the hook, reels, and sees a catch summary. The experience must be designed for hands-free/head-up AR rather than a floating website.

## MVP acceptance flow
1. On launch, show a short instruction and an anchored water/fishing target.
2. One simple supported input casts a line into the target.
3. A visible bobber and bite cue appear.
4. A second supported input hooks the fish.
5. An understandable reel interaction brings the fish in.
6. Show the caught fish and a replay action.
7. Full flow works in Lens Studio Preview without a phone login, payment, or external API.
