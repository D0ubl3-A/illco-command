## Rule

This file validates product-fit signal only. It does not prove acquisition appetite, active budget, corp-dev timing, or strategic priority. Those must be confirmed through warm intros, partner teams, investor backchannels, corp-dev conversations, and customer overlap.

## Acquisition Story To Test

illcoai.tech becomes acquirable when it is presented as a workflow system that already produces measurable business outcomes, not as a generic AI feature. Every buyer conversation must map to one of these purchase reasons:

1. The buyer wants more agentic workflow depth inside an existing platform.
2. The buyer wants stronger vertical workflow replacement in a category it already sells into.
3. The buyer wants proprietary usage/data feedback loops that improve task quality over time.
4. The buyer wants retention, margin, or usage expansion from an already-integrated workflow.

## Outreach Prioritization Formula

Score each target from 1 to 5.

| Factor | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Integration pull | 25% | Buyer already has an obvious surface where illcoai.tech can live. |
| Workflow pain | 20% | Buyer has customers with an urgent workflow problem illcoai.tech solves. |
| Data advantage | 20% | illcoai.tech has usage/data/process insight that improves over time. |
| Diligence readiness | 20% | Legal, security, reliability, and metrics story can survive first review. |
| Intro probability | 15% | Team can reach a relevant champion or corp-dev path. |

Targets below 3.5 stay in research. Targets at or above 4.0 can only enter founder-led outreach after legal, data provenance, benchmark, and fallback gates reach at least `review_ready`.

## Cut Rules

Cut any target from the active outreach list if none of these can be proven within 7 days:

| Cut reason | Standard |
|---|---|
| No integration path | No believable 30-day product integration or marketplace path exists. |
| No economic buyer | No clear executive owner benefits from the workflow outcome. |
| No measurable outcome | illcoai.tech cannot show cost saved, time saved, error reduced, revenue expanded, or retention improved. |
| No differentiated data loop | The buyer could recreate the capability with a generic agent builder and normal prompts. |
| Diligence risk too high | IP, data rights, security, or model quality gaps would stop diligence. |

## Red-Line Warning

Do not claim any company is likely to acquire illcoai.tech until a real champion, budget owner, strategic sponsor, or corp-dev contact has engaged beyond a generic intro. The correct language is "strategic fit hypothesis" until then.
