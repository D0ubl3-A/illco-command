Clear boundaries

What does broad-format production mean?

VANTA is designed for a broad range of legitimate faceless-video formats. Actual production depends on the requested format, configured tools, source availability, media rights and platform requirements. Copyrighted footage is not promised unless the customer has the rights or another applicable lawful basis for its use.

Why does Custom Pro have a setup fee?

Basic starts from a standard system and may require more customer-led iteration. Custom Pro adds upfront engineering of the channel's visual, editorial and production rules, so the setup work is materially different.

What is Astra Ultra?

Astra Ultra is positioned as VANTA's highest-end production option. Its superiority claim applies only relative to the other production engines VANTA offers, not to every AI system on the market.

Is VANTA affiliated with YouTube?

No. VANTA Studio is an independent experimental service and is not endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube.
