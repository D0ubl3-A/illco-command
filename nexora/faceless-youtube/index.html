Clear boundaries

What does “every type” mean?

The system is designed to support a very broad range of legitimate faceless-video formats. Actual production depends on the requested format, available tools, media rights, source availability and platform requirements. Copyrighted footage is not promised unless the customer has rights or an applicable lawful basis to use it.

Can every channel look different?

Yes. The product is positioned around channel-specific brand and production rules rather than forcing every customer into one visual template.

Is this affiliated with YouTube?

No. VANTA Studio is an independent experimental service and is not endorsed by or affiliated with YouTube.
