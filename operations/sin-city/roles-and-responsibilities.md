# Roles and Responsibilities

- **Aaron / Product owner:** approve scope, source rights, production authorization, and irreversible releases.
- **AI Project Kickoff Builder:** maintain charter, ledger, batches, gates, risks, decisions, validation, and continuity.
- **Evidence steward:** verify provenance, coordinate contracts, source registry, and immutable checksums.
- **Geometry pipeline:** register bounds/neighbors and join terrain, parcels, roads, and buildings only from approved evidence.
- **Asset pipeline:** generate or attach architecture/context/binary assets only after evidence approval.
- **QA/release:** apply seam, 50-issue, regression, package, and production-gate checks.

No role may convert a blocked or unknown gate into PASS without direct evidence.
